Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026.

What we collect

When you connect a social media account, Darkflix receives only the access tokens and basic profile information (account name and ID) needed to publish the videos you approve. We do not collect data from your followers.

How we use it

Tokens are used only to upload and publish content that you explicitly approve. They are stored locally on the owner's computer and are never sold or shared with third parties.

Retention and deletion

You can revoke access at any time in the platform's settings. To request deletion of stored tokens and data, email us and we will delete them within 30 days.
