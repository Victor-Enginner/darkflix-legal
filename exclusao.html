Data Deletion Instructions

Darkflix stores only the access tokens and basic profile information (account name and ID) of accounts connected by their owner.

How to delete your data

  1. Remove Darkflix from your account: Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites → remove "clipper/Darkflix". On Instagram: Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites. On TikTok: Settings → Security → Manage app permissions.
  2. Or email victorengenheiroai@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion". All stored tokens and data will be deleted within 30 days and you will receive a confirmation.
